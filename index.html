The problem we solve

Marketing does not need more disconnected activity. It needs a clearer system.

Disconnected activity,
resolved into one route

  1. 01Research may sit in a presentation without changing the strategy.
  2. 02Strategy may not guide the content.
  3. 03Media may generate attention without contributing to a commercial objective.
  4. 04Reports may present numbers without explaining what should happen next.

Rainforest connects every part of the marketing journey, so each decision works towards the same destination. We begin with the business challenge. We understand the market and the people within it. We identify the most valuable opportunity. We build the strategy, execute it across the right touchpoints and use performance data to make the next decision stronger.

Our approach at a glance — 7 building blocks

From business challenge to measurable action

We break complex marketing systems into clear, manageable building blocks. Each block answers a critical question and prepares the ground for the next.

01

Define the destination

What does the business need marketing to achieve?

We clarify the objective, commercial context, priorities, constraints, success measures and the decisions marketing must influence.

02

Decode the landscape

Where can the brand compete more effectively?

We examine the market, category, competitors, culture, customer behaviour and emerging trends to identify the forces shaping demand.

03

Understand the audience

Who needs to think, feel or act differently?

We move beyond broad demographics to uncover motivations, barriers, decision roles, moments of influence and the journey towards action.

04

Find the opportunity

What can the brand own or do differently?

We turn information into a strategic choice: a clear position, value proposition, communication direction and set of marketing priorities.

05

Build the connected plan

Which combination of activity will move the audience?

We connect content, media, platforms, experiences and technology around the objective, audience and decision journey.

06

Bring it to life

How will the strategy appear in the real world?

We create and manage the campaigns, content, media, websites, digital experiences and supporting assets required for execution.

07

Measure and improve

What is working, why is it working and what should happen next?

We monitor the right signals, interpret performance, optimise activity and convert data into clear recommendations.

How this works in practice

Different challenges. The same connected thinking.

B2C application

Turning awareness into action

The challenge
A consumer brand needs to grow consideration and sales in a competitive category without spreading its budget too thinly.
Our response
Identify the highest-value audience and occasions, examine category behaviour and build a connected plan across content, social, search, video, influence and conversion touchpoints.
What we measure
Quality of reach, engagement with priority messages, traffic behaviour, conversion signals and cost efficiency.
B2B application

Turning expertise into opportunity

The challenge
A specialist business has strong technical capability but low market visibility and a long buying cycle involving several decision-makers.
Our response
Map the buying group, identify the proof each stakeholder requires and build an authority-led programme across thought leadership, search, LinkedIn, industry media and events.
What we measure
Engagement from priority accounts, content quality signals, qualified enquiries and contribution to pipeline.

Measurement philosophy

Numbers tell us what happened. Analysis tells us what to do next.

We do not treat reporting as the final stage of a campaign. Measurement is built into the strategy from the beginning. We agree what success should look like, identify the signals that matter at each stage of the journey and create a practical way to monitor them.

Then we interpret the results in context — audience, creative, placement, timing, frequency, cost, traffic quality and conversion behaviour — before recommending what to continue, change, test or stop.
