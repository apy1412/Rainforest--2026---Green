Our Approach
Seven building blocks

02 / 08

A clear way through complex marketing challenges.

See the seven stages↓

Every assignment is different. The discipline behind solving it should not be. Our approach connects seven building blocks, from defining the objective to using evidence to improve the next decision.

Objective — Insight — Strategy — Action

We start upstream

Many marketing conversations begin too late.

Which channel should we use? How much content should we create? What should the campaign look like?

We begin earlier. What is the business trying to change? What is happening in the market? Whose decision matters? What prevents action? What can the brand credibly own? Only then do we determine the right message, experience, content and channel mix.

This prevents activity from becoming the strategy and gives every executional decision a reason to exist.

01Stage one of seven

Define the destination

Begin with the business outcome.

The first task is to create a shared definition of the problem and the progress required. A request for awareness, leads, engagement or sales often contains a more specific underlying need. We work with the client to uncover it. The output is a focused brief, agreed priorities, a measurement direction and a clear view of the decisions the work must enable.

  • The commercial objective and business context
  • The product, service or proposition being supported
  • The markets, audiences and timeframes involved
  • Previous activity, performance and organisational learning
  • Budget, operational and regulatory constraints
  • The role marketing is expected to play
  • The measures that will indicate progress
Capabilities appliedConsultation, workshops, marketing audits, objective setting, KPI frameworks and stakeholder alignment
02Stage two of seven

Decode the landscape

Understand the world in which the decision will be made.

A brand never competes in isolation. It competes against established expectations, familiar alternatives, cultural behaviours, changing technology and the audience's limited attention. We investigate the forces most relevant to the assignment rather than collecting research for its own sake. The output is a landscape of pressures and possibilities, with a clear distinction between market facts, working hypotheses and opportunities that require validation.

  • Market size, maturity and direction
  • Category dynamics and purchase drivers
  • Competitor positioning, messaging, activity and share of voice
  • Search demand and digital behaviour
  • Social and content patterns
  • Cultural and regional considerations
  • Media consumption and channel economics
  • Regulation, technology and emerging trends
Capabilities appliedMarket research, category analysis, competitor benchmarking, social and search intelligence, trend analysis and channel audits
03Stage three of seven

Understand the audience

See the people behind the segment.

Targeting tells us who can be reached. Understanding tells us how they may be moved. We identify the people who make, influence or enable the decision — what they are trying to achieve, what makes them hesitate, what proof they trust and which moments create an opportunity for the brand to matter.

  • Consumer: needs, attitudes, interests and behaviours
  • Consumer: purchase occasions and usage moments
  • Consumer: barriers to trial, conversion or repeat purchase
  • B2B: decision-makers, users, specifiers, influencers and gatekeepers
  • B2B: approval processes and procurement requirements
  • B2B: information and proof required at each stage
Capabilities appliedSegmentation, personas, customer journeys, buying-group maps, behavioural insight and media audience analysis
04Stage four of seven

Find the strategic opportunity

Turn understanding into a choice.

Strategy is not a collection of observations. It is a decision about where to focus and how to win. We bring the business objective, market reality and audience truth together to identify the most valuable role the brand can play. The output is a strategic platform that acts as a decision filter — it helps everyone involved judge whether an idea, channel, asset or activity serves the objective.

  • The strategic opportunity or problem to solve
  • The priority audience and desired change
  • Positioning and value proposition
  • Reasons to believe and supporting proof
  • Communication strategy and message hierarchy
  • Marketing or content pillars
  • The principles that should guide execution
Capabilities appliedPositioning, value propositions, communication strategy, messaging architecture, campaign platforms and content pillars
05Stage five of seven

Build the connected plan

Design a system, not a list of channels.

The plan translates the strategic opportunity into coordinated activity. We assign each channel and touchpoint a specific job according to the audience journey — one may create awareness, another build understanding, another capture active demand and another remove friction at the point of action.

  • Journey and touchpoint strategy
  • Integrated media architecture
  • Channel roles and investment priorities
  • Content themes, formats and cadence
  • Campaign phases and market sequencing
  • Website, landing page or e-commerce requirements
  • Lead capture, nurturing and sales enablement
  • Testing, optimisation and measurement plans
Capabilities appliedIntegrated planning, media strategy, content planning, experience design, campaign architecture, SEO, search, social, programmatic and offline media
06Stage six of seven

Bring the strategy to life

Make every execution part of the same idea.

Execution is where strategic clarity becomes visible. We translate the direction into the content, creative, media, platforms and experiences required to reach the market — maintaining consistency between the message and its many expressions while adapting each asset to the behaviour and requirements of the platform.

  • Campaign concepts and visual systems
  • Copy, design, social content and video
  • Media planning, buying and campaign management
  • Search, social, video and programmatic advertising
  • Television, radio, cinema, print and out-of-home
  • Websites, landing pages, e-commerce and content platforms
  • Campaign toolkits, sales materials and event support
Capabilities appliedCreative development, production, media activation, digital campaign management, web development and marketing operations
07Stage seven of seven

Measure, learn and improve

Make the next decision better than the last.

Performance is monitored against the objective and interpreted across the complete journey. We look beyond surface metrics to understand quality, efficiency and contribution. We turn analysis into recommendations organised around four actions.

ContinueProtect activity that is delivering valuable progress.
ImproveAddress friction, waste or underperformance.
TestResolve important uncertainties through structured experiments.
StopRemove activity that no longer justifies investment.
Capabilities appliedAnalytics, dashboards, attribution, conversion tracking, campaign optimisation, performance reviews and strategic recommendations.

Flexible by design

Use the full journey or begin where the need is greatest.

Some clients need a complete marketing strategy and execution partner. Others need a focused answer: a market entry assessment, a media plan, a campaign platform, a website, an analytics framework or an independent review of current performance.

The building-block approach allows us to enter at the right point while protecting the connection to the wider objective.
