Our Work
Thinking, not just output

06 / 08

The work is more than the output.

It is the thinking that connects it.

Our work begins with a challenge and follows the decisions that turn it into action. Each story shows the objective, insight, strategic choice, connected execution, measurement and learning — not simply a gallery of finished assets.

Objective — Insight — Decision — Outcome

How we tell a story

Every case study follows the same disciplined structure

Approved client stories are published as they are confirmed. In the meantime, here is the structure every Rainforest story follows — from objective to outcome.

S—01

The objective

What did the business need to achieve, and why did it matter?

S—02

The challenge

What market, audience, competitive, operational or measurement issue stood in the way?

S—03

What we learned

What insight changed the direction or helped focus the opportunity?

S—04

The strategic decision

What did Rainforest choose to prioritise, and what did it deliberately avoid?

S—05

The connected plan

How did message, content, media, technology and the customer journey work together?

S—06

Execution

What was created, launched or managed?

S—07

Measurement

Which measures were agreed, and how was performance monitored?

S—08

Outcome and learning

What changed, what did the evidence reveal, and what was recommended next?
