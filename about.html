Why Rainforest exists

Clients are often left to connect the specialisms.

The marketing industry has developed deep specialisms, but clients are often left to connect them. Research, brand strategy, creative, media, development and analytics may be handled by different teams, under different briefs and measured in different ways. The result can be a great deal of activity without a shared view of the business objective.

Rainforest exists to close those gaps. We create the strategic thread that runs from the initial challenge to the final recommendation — helping internal teams and specialist partners work from the same understanding, and giving the client a clearer basis for making decisions.

What we believe

Five principles that shape every engagement.

  1. P—01

    The objective comes before the channel.

    A platform, format or technology is useful only when it has a clear role in solving the problem.

  2. P—02

    Simplicity is the result of rigorous thinking.

    Complexity should be understood, not passed on to the client. We break it into useful decisions, priorities and actions.

  3. P—03

    Integration requires more than using many channels.

    True integration means that insight, strategy, content, media, experience and measurement reinforce one another.

  4. P—04

    Evidence should lead to action.

    Reporting has value when it helps a team decide what to continue, improve, test or stop.

  5. P—05

    Experience matters most when it remains adaptable.

    More than 25 years in media and marketing gives us perspective. Curiosity, collaboration and a willingness to challenge assumptions keep that experience relevant.

How we work with clients

Four commitments behind every engagement.

01

Senior thinking, close to the work

Strategy should not disappear during execution. We keep the people shaping the direction connected to the decisions that bring it to market.

02

Built around the challenge

We shape the team and scope around what the assignment requires, bringing together the relevant strategic, creative, media, technology and analytics expertise.

03

Collaborative, but clear

We work closely with client teams and partners, while remaining direct about priorities, trade-offs and what the evidence suggests.

04

Accountable to the objective

We agree how progress will be judged and keep recommendations connected to the outcome defined at the beginning.
